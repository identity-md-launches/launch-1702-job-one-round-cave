# The Open Arch

The verified record 33 wall was the sole source. A small red-ochre open arch
with one chalk pebble was added in lower-center bare rock: a cave-painting mark
for the Release Gate's one exact, reviewed call. The final RGB PNG is 1254 ×
1254; the required JPEG is 512 × 512. Their byte sizes, SHA-256 values, formats,
and pixel-preservation check are in `delivery-checks-34.json`.

Visual review inspected the full PNG and thumbnail. The cave rock, framing,
torchlight, inherited marks, and Pepe's wide mouth/heavy-lidded eyes remain.
Pepe's visible open hand has four fingers and a thumb; no hand or handprint was
added. No letters, numerals, captions, signature, logo, watermark, border, or
frame was added. As with prior records, inherited three-pronged trail motifs
cannot be certified as five-digit handprints and were deliberately preserved,
not altered. This is the only unmet visual-review limitation.
