# Gathering 14 visual review

**Mark:** The Reviewed Gate. Three chalk-white pebbles pass a red-ochre arch beside Pepe, representing the exact reviewed call and evidence joining at a gate. One image-tool render made the motif using the downloaded, hash-verified Gathering 13 wall as its reference. No new hand or Pepe was added.

**Conservation:** the initial whole-wall edit softened inherited rock. Its generated rock and ancestral rendering were discarded. `finish_wall_14.py` transferred only actual generated red/chalk pigment into the previously bare patch `[310,548,413,675]`, blending the underlying stone through it. All pixels outside the patch are byte-for-byte equal to the ancestor raster. 4,944 pixels changed; 4,480 ochre and 464 chalk pigment samples were used. The original file hash, generated render hash and final hash are recorded in `visual-checks.json`. The script is intentionally a one-time operation and refuses a different/already-finished ancestor.

**Outputs:** `artifacts/gathering/wall-v14.png` and the identical requested `wall.png` mirror are 1254 × 1254 opaque RGB 8-bit PNGs, 3,920,901 bytes each. `dist/gathering/14.jpg` is a 512 × 512 baseline JPEG, 54,700 bytes. PNG CRCs/raster and JPEG structure/dimensions were checked with the image inspection tool. Both final images were visually inspected.

**Visual review:** the warm torchlit rock, cracks, moss, light and square framing remain. Every earlier painting remains, including the hourglass, bowls, pebble chains, face, forked trails and central Pepe. His heavy-lidded eyes and wide mouth identify the inherited cave Pepe. I counted four fingers and one thumb on each hand: five left, five right. No new hands or stencils were added. The new pigment is worn and uneven; stone shows through. No letters, numbers, words, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, borders, political/hateful symbols or real people were observed.

**Unmet visual requirements observed:** none. Motif identification and digit counting are subjective contributor review; exact conservation is measured outside the addition patch. The ancestor's realistic rock is retained as requested rather than reconstructed. Only the final PNG wall/mirror and its requested JPEG are retained as images; no drafts or preview images were saved in source folders.
